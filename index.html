Working duplicate triage

Similar reviews need a second look.

A small deterministic matcher compares clinic, date, procedure, and distinctive words in five collected English summaries. Procedure labels here are reviewed annotations. The result is a review queue; it does not merge records or claim that two accounts belong to one patient.

Why this matters: repeated syndicated accounts can inflate review counts. Clinic, date, and wording are useful screening signals, but a human needs original-post identifiers or stronger provenance before merging records. Inspect the public listing ↗

How this would scale

A narrow layer between translation and trust.

1. Extract with citations

Map each field to a source passage, retain the original language and page URL, and allow the model to abstain.

2. Check the risky claims

Human review for surgeon identity, procedure verification, conflicting time points, and anything that changes a buyer’s interpretation.

3. Display limits openly

Show “not mentioned” and “not independently verified.” Evidence coverage is not a clinic score or a medical recommendation.
